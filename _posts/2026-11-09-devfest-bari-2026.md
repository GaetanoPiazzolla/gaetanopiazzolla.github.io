> **Beyond Naive RAG: Grounding LLMs with Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG in Action**

## Abstract

Classic RAG built solely on a Vector Database works well in demos, but quickly shows its limits in production: context fragmentation, inability to connect information distributed across multiple documents (*multi-hop reasoning*), and hallucinations when semantic similarity alone is not enough.

In this practical session, we will see how to overcome these limits by pairing vectors with the explicit structure of a **Knowledge Graph**.

Through code examples and an end-to-end demo, we will explore:

1. **The structural limits of Vector-only RAG** and why embeddings alone lose domain relationships.
2. **Building a Knowledge Graph with LLMs**: extracting entities and relationships from unstructured text, managing the MetaGraph (`Document -> Chunk -> Entity`), and disambiguation.
3. **GraphRAG Architecture and Hybrid Retrieval**: combining Vector Index and graph traversal to provide the LLM with a rich, deterministic, and traceable context.
4. **Text2Cypher, Schema Injection, and AI Agent integration** via the **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**.

Attendees will walk away with concrete architectural patterns, queries, and ready-to-use code to turn a fragile RAG pipeline into a truly domain-aware system.
